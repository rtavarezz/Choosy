# Choosy Community Code of Conduct

## Our Mission

Choosy is building the future of group decision-making technology. Our community values innovation, collaboration, and creating meaningful connections through shared experiences.

## Our Pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Choosy community a positive, professional, and productive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

### Professional Behavior
- **Respectful Communication**: Use welcoming and inclusive language
- **Constructive Feedback**: Focus on behaviors and code, not personal characteristics
- **Technical Excellence**: Strive for high-quality contributions and thoughtful discussions
- **Innovation Focus**: Encourage creative solutions and forward-thinking approaches

### Collaborative Environment  
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Help others learn and grow
- **Open Communication**: Be transparent about challenges and decisions
- **Credit Recognition**: Acknowledge others' contributions appropriately
- **Inclusive Participation**: Welcome newcomers and diverse perspectives

### Intellectual Property Respect
- **Original Contributions**: Ensure all contributions are your original work or properly licensed
- **Proprietary Awareness**: Respect Choosy's intellectual property and trade secrets
- **Fair Competition**: Do not use insider knowledge for competing products
- **License Compliance**: Follow all licensing terms and agreements

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, trolling, or discriminatory comments
- Personal attacks or ad hominem arguments
- Sharing proprietary algorithms or business logic outside the project
- Attempting to reverse engineer or copy Choosy's core innovations
- Publishing private communications without consent
- Using the community for spam or self-promotion unrelated to Choosy
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Business and Legal Conduct

Given Choosy's commercial potential and intellectual property value:
- Respect confidential information shared in development discussions
- Do not solicit contributors for competing projects
- Report security vulnerabilities through proper channels (see SECURITY.md)
- Comply with all contributor agreements and licensing terms

## Reporting and Enforcement

### Reporting Process
Report violations by contacting the maintainer directly through:
- GitHub issues (for public technical discussions)
- Direct contact for sensitive matters
- Security vulnerabilities through responsible disclosure

### Consequences
Violations may result in:
1. **Warning**: Private feedback and guidance
2. **Temporary Suspension**: Limited participation for a specified period
3. **Permanent Ban**: Removal from community and contributor access revocation
4. **Legal Action**: For intellectual property violations or malicious behavior

### Appeals Process
Community members may appeal enforcement actions by:
- Providing additional context or clarification
- Demonstrating corrective actions taken
- Requesting mediation for misunderstandings

## Community Leadership

Maintainers are responsible for:
- Setting technical direction and standards
- Protecting intellectual property and trade secrets
- Fostering an inclusive and innovative environment
- Making final decisions on contributions and community membership

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.0, with additional provisions specific to Choosy's commercial and intellectual property considerations.

## Questions

For questions about this Code of Conduct, contact the project maintainer or open a community discussion issue.
